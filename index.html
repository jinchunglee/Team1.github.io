Hey, We're Team 1

我們正在開發一款錄音教學輔具，旨在幫助身心障礙兒童，為他們提供一種簡單且有效的方式來學習。我們的目標是透過這個簡單而強大的工具，提升這些孩子的獨立性，幫助他們在日常生活中取得顯著的進步與改變。

We are developing an innovative assistive button designed to support children with special needs, providing them with an easy and effective way to communicate and interact with their environment. Our goal is to empower these children by enhancing their independence, offering a simple yet powerful tool that can make a significant difference in their daily lives.

助人為樂，學以致用

我們團隊致力於利用科技為社會帶來正面的影響。我們是電機系的學生，運用所學的專業知識與技能，設計出專為身心障礙兒童量身打造的輔助工具。 這個專案的核心目標是開發創新且實用的設備，幫助這些孩子克服日常生活中的挑戰，提升他們與外界互動與溝通的能力，並增強他們的獨立性。我們深入了解他們所面臨的困難，設計出簡單易用且符合他們需求的解決方案。 我們的使命是透過科技，打破限制與障礙，讓每一位孩子都能在生活中找到自信，享受學習與成長的樂趣。我們相信，透過這些輔助工具，可以為他們帶來實質的改變，讓他們在充滿愛與支持的環境中茁壯成長。 讓我們一起攜手創造一個更加包容的世界，讓科技真正服務每一個人。

Our team is dedicated to using technology to create a positive impact on society. As students from the Electrical Engineering Department, we have applied our knowledge and skills to design assistive tools specifically tailored for children with disabilities. The primary goal of this project is to develop innovative and practical devices that empower these children to overcome daily challenges, enhance their communication and interaction with the world, and improve their independence. By understanding the difficulties they face, we design solutions that are simple, user-friendly, and customized to their needs. Our mission is to use technology to break barriers and limitations, enabling every child to gain confidence, enjoy learning, and experience growth. We believe that these assistive tools can make a tangible difference in their lives, helping them thrive in a supportive and inclusive environment. Join us in building a more inclusive world where technology truly serves everyone.
